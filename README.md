# On a critical acceleration scale of dark matter in ΛCDM and dynamical dark energy: plotted data and manuscript

Xu Z., 2025, *On a critical acceleration scale of dark matter in ΛCDM and dynamical dark energy*, ApJ 981, 40, [doi:10.3847/1538-4357/adaeb3](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/adaeb3); arXiv: [2203.05606](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.05606).

## Major findings

- In the Virgo N-body simulation the acceleration fluctuation of halo particles about their halo's mean has an RMS of a_c = 1.2×10⁻¹⁰ m/s² at z = 0, the value of the empirical acceleration a₀ of the baryonic Tully–Fisher relation and MOND.
- The velocity fluctuation of halo particles is u_c = 496 km/s at z = 0, and the energy cascade between haloes proceeds at ε_u ≈ −3×10⁻⁷ m²/s³ (plateau 2.8–3.3×10⁻⁷ m²/s³ for z ≤ 1).
- The three are linked by ε_u ≈ −a_c u_c/[2(3π)²], with the factor 3π from the angle at which a merging halo enters; the simulation gives −a_c u_c/ε_u = 189 at z = 0, against 2(3π)² = 178.
- The relation predicts a_c ∝ (1+z)^{3/4} (∝ t^{−1/2} in the matter era); the simulation gives a_c ∝ a^{−0.69}, the EAGLE simulation a₀ ∝ a^{−0.75}, the Magneticum simulation a₀ ∝ a^{−0.98}; MOND with a₀ ∝ cH would give a^{−3/2}.
- Fitting log M_b = α log v_f − γ log(1+z) + β₀ to 253 galaxies (118 at z = 0, 135 at 0.6 < z < 2.5) gives γ = 0.89 for α = 4 (95 per cent range 0.69–1.10), consistent with 3/4 and below the MOND value 3/2; the positive γ comes from the offset between the local and the high-redshift samples.
- The deep-MOND force law F = m a²/a_c follows for baryons that share the cascade rate of dark matter and whose effective kinetic energy grows linearly with speed, as in the maximum-entropy velocity distribution of dark matter.
- The energy density a_c²/G ≈ 2×10⁻¹⁰ J/m³ is of the order of the dark-energy density (5×10⁻¹⁰ J/m³); a dark-energy model built on the acceleration fluctuation of all particles is constant in the matter era and decreases as a^{−1/2} when halo growth stops.
